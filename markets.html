Expected move = price × implied volatility × √time, with volatility backed out of the at-the-money straddle. Daily uses one trading day (IV ÷ 16); weekly and monthly use the time left until expiry. That's a one-standard-deviation range: the market prices roughly a 68% chance of finishing between Low and High. During market hours the daily range is centered on the previous close. Used shows how far the live price has traveled toward the edge of the range (±100% = at Low/High). Weekly runs through this week's last expiry; monthly runs through the next standard monthly expiry (third Friday). Names without weekly options (marked M) use the next monthly. Ranges refresh every 30 min during market hours. Live prices from Finnhub, options data from Yahoo Finance. Not investment advice.
