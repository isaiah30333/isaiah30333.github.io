Expected move = price × at-the-money implied volatility × √(trading days ÷ 252). That's a one-standard-deviation range: the market prices roughly a 68% chance of finishing between Low and High. During market hours the daily range is centered on the previous close. Used shows how far the live price has traveled toward the edge of the range (±100% = at Low/High). Weekly runs through this week's last expiry. Names without weekly options (marked M) use the next monthly. Ranges refresh every 30 min during market hours.
