About me

I'm finishing a Business Finance degree at Colorado State University with a concentration in Investment Analysis, graduating in December 2026. After graduation I plan to start the CFA program, with the CMT to follow.

At CSU I'm an analyst in the Pioneer Student Investment Fund, a student-run portfolio of about $600K benchmarked against the S&P 500, taught in the same class as the Summit Fund. I'm on the consumer staples sector team. I pitched Unilever to the fund and helped build our team's pitch on Monster Beverage, using a DCF, comparable company multiples, and our own growth projections. In the Fall 2026 market update I presented portfolio performance, walking the class through our returns against the S&P 500 and the Bloomberg attribution behind them. I'm also on the fund's Recruitment Committee, and I gave the recruiting presentation to more than 200 students.

In my Enterprise Valuation class, I built the industry regression for our group's valuation of Johnson & Johnson. It regresses forward P/E on expected revenue growth and EBITDA margin across 14 pharma and MedTech peers to estimate the multiple J&J's fundamentals actually support, instead of assuming it deserves the peer median.

Outside of class, I've traded SPX and SPY options on my own account since 2021. Watching how much movement the options market prices in each day is what led me to build the Expected Moves tool on this site.

I also worked in the golf shop and cart barn at Walking Stick Golf Course in Pueblo, ringing up purchases and keeping check-ins moving for 50+ customers a day.

Skills and tools: DCF valuation · Comparable multiples · Options and implied volatility · Bloomberg Terminal · TradingView · Excel
